Not at all. We perform implants under local anesthesia — you feel no pain during the procedure. Post-surgery, mild discomfort is managed with prescribed painkillers. Most patients return to normal activities within 1-2 days. Our advanced techniques including flapless surgery and PRF (Platelet Rich Fibrin) ensure faster healing.

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. The titanium fixture fuses with your jawbone (osseointegration) becoming a permanent part of your body. The crown on top may need replacement after 10-15 years due to normal wear. We provide a 15-year warranty on Nobel Biocare implants and a lifetime warranty on Straumann.

Yes! We are empaneled with all major insurance providers including ICICI Lombard, Star Health, HDFC Ergo, Bajaj Allianz, New India Assurance, and many more. Our insurance desk handles all paperwork. Cashless facility is available for most corporate tie-ups. We also offer 0% EMI options through Bajaj Finserv and Simpl.

We completely understand dental anxiety. We offer conscious sedation (laughing gas), oral sedation, and IV sedation for anxious patients. Our clinic is designed to be calming — with music, aromatherapy, and warm blankets. We also offer the "gentle start" program where your first visit is just a conversation, no treatment.

We recommend a check-up and cleaning every 6 months for most adults. If you have gum disease, diabetes, or are undergoing orthodontic treatment, every 3-4 months. Children should have their first dental visit by age 1, then every 6 months. Regular visits help catch problems early when they're simpler and cheaper to treat.
