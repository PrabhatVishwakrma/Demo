About Us

A Legacy of Compassion and Change

Founded in 2009, HopeRise Foundation began with a simple yet powerful vision — to ensure that no child is deprived of basic education, healthcare, and the opportunity to thrive. What started as a small group of passionate individuals in a single room has grown into a movement that touches over 50,000 lives across 120 communities.

We believe that sustainable change comes not from charity alone, but from empowering communities to become self-reliant. Our holistic approach addresses the root causes of poverty through education programs, healthcare initiatives, women's empowerment, skill development, and environmental sustainability projects.

Our Mission

To empower underserved communities through quality education, accessible healthcare, and sustainable livelihood programs.

Our Vision

A world where every individual has equal access to opportunities, resources, and the chance to lead a dignified life.

What We Do

Our Services & Interventions

We provide comprehensive, multi-dimensional support to communities in need. Each of our programs is designed to create sustainable, long-term impact rather than temporary relief.

Education & Scholarships

Providing free quality education, school supplies, digital literacy programs, and merit-based scholarships to underprivileged children from primary through higher education.

  • After-school tutoring centers
  • Digital learning labs
  • Career counseling workshops
Learn More

Healthcare & Nutrition

Running free medical camps, maternal health programs, immunization drives, and nutrition support to ensure healthy communities where no one suffers from preventable diseases.

  • Monthly health check-up camps
  • Malnutrition rehabilitation
  • Mental health counseling
Learn More

Women Empowerment

Enabling women through self-help groups, vocational training, micro-finance support, and awareness campaigns to become economically independent and socially empowered.

  • Self-help group formation
  • Tailoring & handicraft training
  • Legal rights awareness
Learn More

Shelter & Rehabilitation

Providing safe shelter, rehabilitation, and reintegration support for homeless individuals, orphaned children, and survivors of abuse and human trafficking.

  • Children's shelter homes
  • De-addiction centers
  • Family reintegration support
Learn More

Environment & Sustainability

Promoting environmental conservation through tree plantation drives, water conservation projects, waste management programs, and climate change awareness.

  • 100,000+ trees planted
  • Rainwater harvesting systems
  • Solar energy installations
Learn More

Skill Development & Livelihood

Equipping youth and adults with market-relevant skills through vocational training, entrepreneurship development, and job placement assistance.
