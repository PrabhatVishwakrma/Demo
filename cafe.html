Our Story

A small roastery with a big heart.

Brûlée & Co. began as a corner shop with a single second-hand roaster and a stubborn belief: that coffee should be slow, honest, and made by people who care. A decade later, we still roast every bean in-house, bake every croissant before sunrise, and learn the names of every regular.

We partner directly with growers in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala — paying above fair-trade so the people behind the cup are paid what they're worth.

From Bean to Cup

Four steps. No shortcuts.

01

Source

We travel to origin countries and meet growers face-to-face. Relationships first, beans second.

02

Roast

Small-batch roasted every Tuesday in our 1960s Probat. Light, balanced, never bitter.

03

Grind

Ground to order on Mahlkönig grinders, dialled in by our baristas every morning.

04

Pour

Pulled on La Marzocco, poured with care, served in warm ceramics. Then it's yours.
