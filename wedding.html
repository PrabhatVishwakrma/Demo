Our Story

Crafting Love Stories Since 2012

Eternal Vows was born from a passion for turning ordinary moments into extraordinary memories. Founded by Priya and Arjun Mehra, our studio has grown from a small two-person team into one of India's most sought-after luxury wedding planning companies.

We believe every couple deserves a wedding that's as unique as their love story. Our approach combines meticulous planning, creative design, and flawless execution to deliver celebrations that exceed expectations. From the first consultation to the last dance, we handle every detail so you can simply enjoy your special day.

Common Questions

We recommend booking 8-12 months in advance for a full wedding, and 4-6 months for partial planning or day-of coordination. However, we've also pulled off beautiful weddings in 2-3 months when schedules aligned. The earlier you book, the more time we have to secure the best vendors and venues, especially for peak wedding season (October-March).

We work across all budget ranges — from intimate ₹3 lakh celebrations to ultra-luxury multi-crore destination weddings. We believe great design isn't about how much you spend, but how creatively you use your budget. During our initial consultation, we'll understand your budget and recommend the best approach to maximize value without compromising on the experience.
