Our Studio

Where Vision Meets Craftsmanship

ATELIER was founded in 2014 by Arjun Khanna with a singular vision — to create interiors that are both breathtaking and deeply personal. What started as a one-person studio has grown into a team of 30+ designers, architects, and project managers.

We believe great design isn't about following trends — it's about understanding how you live, work, and dream. Every project begins with listening, and ends with spaces that feel effortlessly yours.
