About Us

We Don't Just Trek, We Transform

TrailBlazers was born from a campfire conversation between three mountaineering friends who believed that the mountains change people for the better. Since 2016, we've guided over 15,000 adventurers through some of India's most challenging and beautiful terrain.

Every trek is meticulously planned with safety as the non-negotiable priority. Our team includes certified mountaineers, wilderness first responders, and local experts who know every rock and stream of the trails we operate on.

Frequently Asked Questions

Absolutely! We have specially designed "Easy" category treks perfect for first-timers. Our Hampta Pass, Kodachadri, and Valley of Flowers treks are beginner-friendly. We also send a pre-trek training plan 4 weeks before the trek to help you prepare physically.

30+ days before trek: 90% refund. 15-30 days: 50% refund. 7-15 days: 25% refund. Less than 7 days: No refund. However, you can transfer your seat to a friend at no extra cost up to 48 hours before the trek. We also offer TrekShield — our flexible rebooking add-on for ₹499.

We provide tents, sleeping bags, sleeping mats, cooking equipment, first aid kits, and safety gear. You need to carry personal clothing, trekking shoes, a backpack (40-50L), water bottles, headlamp, and personal medications. A detailed packing list is shared upon booking.

Every trek has at least one Wilderness First Responder (WFR) certified guide. We carry a comprehensive medical kit including altitude sickness medication (Diamox), oxygen cylinders (for high-altitude treks), and a portable altitude chamber. We have satellite phones for communication and pre-arranged evacuation routes to the nearest medical facility.

All meals from Day 1 breakfast to last day lunch are included. Our kitchen team prepares fresh, nutritious meals — both vegetarian and non-vegetarian options. We provide purified drinking water throughout the trek. Special dietary requirements (vegan, Jain, gluten-free) can be accommodated with prior notice.
